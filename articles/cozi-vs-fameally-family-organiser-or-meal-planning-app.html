Cozi vs Fameally: Family Organiser or Meal Planning App?

Compare the whole household week: the calendar, the dinner decision and the list somebody else takes shopping.

By Edward Nickless · Fameally Published 23 November 2026 10 minute read

The club finish time has changed, dinner needs moving and somebody else is doing the shopping. A useful family app should help the people involved find the new plan without one person explaining it three times.

Cozi is worth considering if you want family appointments, chores, meals and lists together. Fameally is worth considering if your calendar already works and you want a routine centred on saved meals, weekly plans and the household shop. Cozi also includes meal planning and tools for adding recipe ingredients to shopping lists, so compare how each app fits your week.

About this comparison: Fameally is our app. We checked current official Cozi information and Fameally's published features on 23 November 2026. This is a comparison of documentation, not a side-by-side hands-on test of both apps. The household week below is a suggested trial, not a report of measured results.

Start with the coordination job

Think about the last change that made your week harder. Did somebody miss an appointment? Was the dinner still based on an evening when everyone would be home? Or did the person shopping have an older list?

Write down the two problems that happen most often. Then decide what you want the app to hold. A household that needs one view of school events and appointments has a different starting point from a couple who already share a calendar but keep rebuilding dinner plans in messages.

  • Schedules are the sticking point: look closely at Cozi's family calendar and how it connects with meals.
  • Choosing and repeating dinners is the sticking point: look closely at Fameally's saved meals and weekly planning routine.
  • The shopping handover is the sticking point: compare both list workflows with the person who will actually shop.

If you already use Cozi successfully for family life, try its food-planning features before adding another app. If you already have a reliable shared calendar, include the effort of moving schedules when deciding whether you need a wider family organiser.

Compare calendars, meals and chores

Cozi brings the household schedule into the food decision

Cozi's shared calendar uses colours for family members and supports events, reminders and views of different people's schedules. Its Meal Planner shows how busy the days are, lets you choose recipes and puts planned meals on the family calendar. The Recipe Box and ordinary meal planner are included in Cozi Free.

That can be a useful starting point when the dinner decision depends on who is out and when they return. You still choose a meal that fits the evening; seeing a busy day does not decide your household's preferences for you.

Cozi also documents chore lists for each family member. If “who is clearing up?” belongs in the same place as the week, include that in your trial.

Fameally starts with the meals and the household shop

Fameally lets you save familiar meals, choose dinners for the week and build a list from their ingredients. Individual and Family access include saved weekly plans to reuse. The first meal plan and shopping list tutorial shows the basic connection between a meal, its place in the week and the food list.

Use the calendar your household already checks to establish the busy evenings, then choose meals in Fameally to fit them. Keep appointments in that calendar and agree who is cooking and shopping when you choose the meals.

For households with existing Google, Apple or Outlook calendars, check Cozi's calendar connection guide. It describes read-only calendar feeds in either direction. Follow the plan through to the shopping list

A meal on a date and a useful shopping list are related jobs. Include the move between them in your comparison rather than stopping when both apps display dinner.

Cozi's shopping-list guidance describes sending a recipe's ingredients to a new or existing list. Its meal-planning page shows those ingredients grouped beneath the recipe name. You can also keep lists for other shopping jobs.

Fameally builds the food list from planned meals, and matching ingredient names and units can be combined. Add the household extras and check what you already have before shopping. Its current plans distinguish the basic list workflow from features such as weekly essentials and aisle sorting.

Try two dinners that use the same ingredient. Look at whether you prefer seeing the ingredients with their recipes or working from combined quantities. Check the result against the amounts you intend to cook; neither a saved recipe nor a generated list tells you what is physically in the cupboard.

Then change one dinner and inspect the list again. Check what still belongs to another meal, what needs adding and which extras were entered separately. Treat this as a task to try in each app, rather than assuming identical list behaviour from similar feature names.

Check family access and devices

Cozi supports iPhone, Android and web access. Its calendar page describes a family of up to 12 members. The account FAQ explains that members use their included email addresses with one shared family password, and that people given account access can view, add, edit and delete its data. Check that account model suits everyone you intend to include.

Fameally supports iPhone, iPad and Android. With Family access, the owner can invite up to five other people into the shared workspace. Each person signs in to their own account. The household-sharing tutorial covers the owner and invitee setup.

Pick the two people most likely to hand over a shop and check their devices first. Ask each person to find the intended plan and list. A feature is more useful when both people can reach it comfortably, including the person who did not set up the account.

Cozi has a UK App Store listing. Check the current listing for your device and region, particularly before paying for an upgrade. We have not completed a UK subscription purchase as part of this comparison.

Try a changed dinner and a different shopper

Use a week your household recognises. For example, Wednesday's club now finishes at seven, the planned roast no longer fits and Jamie is doing the shop instead of Alex. Decide together to have a familiar omelette-and-toast dinner on Wednesday and move the roast to Sunday.

The example is a prompt for your own trial. Use meals and quantities suitable for your household; it is not a supplied meal plan or an observed test of either product.

  1. Make the changed evening visible. In Cozi, check the club event and choose the meal around that schedule. With Fameally, check the calendar your household uses, then put the agreed dinner into the food plan.
  2. Account for both dinners. Check Wednesday and Sunday so the roast hasn't simply disappeared from the household's intentions.
  3. Review the shopping result. In Cozi, use the documented recipe-to-list action and inspect the chosen list. In Fameally, review the food list from the planned meals. Check eggs and bread, then check which roast ingredients still belong to Sunday.
  4. Add the rest of the shop. Include a real household extra, such as washing-up liquid, so you test more than recipe ingredients.
  5. Hand over to Jamie. Ask the new shopper to find the right list on their device and explain what remains to buy. Agree who will cook and where any further change will be communicated.
  6. Review the routine together. Was the change easy to find? Did anybody need a separate explanation? Did the person shopping prefer the list layout? Keep those answers alongside the subscription cost.

Choose Cozi if its wider view of events, chores and food makes the household easier to coordinate. Choose Fameally if the food-focused routine fits the calendars and conversations you already use. Keeping both is possible, but agree which app holds the dinner decision and which list the shopper follows so you don't maintain two competing versions of the week.

A few common questions

Can Cozi plan meals, or is it just a calendar?
Cozi includes meal tools alongside its calendar. Its feature overview describes recipes, meal scheduling and ingredients sent to shopping lists.
Do I need Cozi Max to plan dinners?
No. Cozi's official meal-planning page includes the ordinary planner in Free. Check Max if you specifically want its AI tools.
Which is better when someone else does the shopping?
Try the handover in both. The useful result is that the intended shopper can find the agreed list, understand quantities and see the changes that affect their shop. A shared-list label alone doesn't tell you which routine your household prefers.
Can I keep my existing calendar and use Fameally?
You can check your existing calendar when choosing meals in Fameally. Agree where schedule changes are recorded and update the food plan accordingly; this is a household routine rather than a claim of an automatic calendar connection.
