Mealime vs Fameally: choosing your next meal planning app

Looking for a Mealime alternative? Compare recipes, reusable weekly plans and shared shopping, and find a routine that fits your household.

By Edward Nickless · Fameally Published 15 October 2026 6 minute read

If you use Mealime, choosing your next meal planning app is about keeping a useful routine going. You might want fresh recipe ideas, a simpler weekly shop, or somewhere to keep the dinners your household already enjoys. Fameally is worth considering when you want your own meals, weekly plans and household shopping connected.

This guide is published by Fameally, developed by ENVentures LTD. The competitor details come from official product and transition information, rather than hands-on testing of Meals Hub.

What's happening to Mealime?

Mealime's official announcement says the app will close on 21 October 2026. Its successor is Meals Hub, available through Albertsons Companies websites and apps. Meals Hub and Fameally are separate services.

Mealime's appeal has been the path from recipe ideas to a meal plan and categorised grocery list. If that helped you cook more often, it makes sense to look for a replacement that keeps the useful parts. The question is which parts matter most in your next ordinary week.

Mealime and Fameally: the practical differences

Both connect meals with shopping. Fameally puts your saved meals and household routine at the centre. Mealime's published offering emphasises personalised recipe planning. Where Fameally fits into your week

Picture Sunday evening. You know Tuesday will be late, somebody else is cooking on Wednesday, and there's already half a bag of rice in the cupboard. You need dinners that fit those evenings, plus a list the shopper can understand.

Start with meals you actually cook

Save your regular chilli, vegetable curry and pasta bake with their ingredients and instructions. You can also import a recipe from a URL and review the result before saving. Check quantities and methods so the saved version matches how you cook it.

You don't need a new recipe for every night. Keeping your household's favourites together means the next plan starts with meals you know will get eaten. Add something new when you fancy it, then keep the ones that work.

Let the plan do some of the list-writing

Put the curry on the busy evening and leave a flexible night for leftovers. Generate the shopping list from the meals you've planned, then check the rice, tins and freezer before you buy. Matching ingredients and units can be combined, which helps when two dinners use the same food.

The rest of the shop belongs here too. Add breakfast basics, packed-lunch food, washing tablets and toothpaste, with household items in their own sections. That makes the list useful for the whole trip, including things that will never appear in a dinner recipe.

Keep useful weeks, and share the work

When a week works, save the plan to reuse on Individual or Family. You can return to a familiar starting point, then adjust it to the next week's commitments. Tuesday doesn't have to become curry night forever.

Core covers saved meals and ingredient-based shopping lists. Individual adds saved weekly plans, one-tap weekly essentials and aisle sorting. Family includes those features and shared meals, plans and lists: the owner can invite up to five people by email, and everyone can update the shared list.

Choose around who needs access. If one person plans and shops, a personal plan may suit you. If someone else needs to check dinner or add the milk, try that shared routine together. See current Fameally plans and UK pricing for the details.

When Meals Hub deserves a look

Meals Hub continues Mealime recipes, adds further recipes, and supports dietary preferences. It is free, and Mealime says you can plan and make lists without shopping at an Albertsons store. Follow the official Meals Hub guidance to explore access.

If keeping those recipes is your priority, explore that route. If you want your own repeatable household routine, try Fameally with the dinners you already cook. For other approaches, see our meal planning apps comparison.

Start your first week in Fameally

Start with one shop rather than rebuilding everything at once:

  1. Save three familiar meals. Add the ingredients you need to shop for.
  2. Put them into your week. Match the easiest dinner to the busiest evening.
  3. Build and check the list. Remove what you already have and add household essentials.
  4. Include the other shopper. With Family access, invite them to check and update the same list.

Eligible new users get 60 days of free Family access, with no payment details required and no automatic charge when it ends. Use that time to find out whether a saved meal makes next week quicker and whether sharing makes the shop easier.

As you record purchases, Due Soon suggestions can help prompt checks for regular items. An item needs at least three recorded purchases in separate shopping cycles, with reasonably consistent gaps. Suggestions don't measure what's left at home; you decide what to add.

A few common questions

Can I move my Mealime account straight into Fameally?

This guide doesn't offer a direct account transfer. Start with your own meals, or import recipe URLs you can access and review. Don't assume a Mealime recipe or account will transfer automatically.

Will Fameally check every recipe against my dietary needs?

Use meals that suit your household and check ingredients yourself. Saving or importing a recipe isn't a guarantee of dietary suitability.

Which Fameally plan includes household sharing?

Family includes the shared workspace. Core and Individual are personal plans; eligible introductory Family access lets you try planning and shopping together.
